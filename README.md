
# PhonePe – Product Teardown

## Project Overview
An independent product teardown of PhonePe, exploring its digital payments ecosystem, product offerings and business strategy.

## Objective
To understand PhonePe's product positioning, user experience, business model and competitive landscape.

## Areas of Analysis
- Product and feature analysis
- Business model understanding
- User experience and value proposition
- Market positioning
